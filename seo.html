Platform properties, not statistics

Five things Shopify does by default that Google reads badly

Every one of these is verifiable on your own store in a browser, right now. None of them is a claim about your rankings.

  1. 01

    Every product lives at two URLs

    /products/x and /collections/y/products/x both serve the same page. Which one Google keeps depends entirely on the canonical tag your theme emits.

  2. 02

    Filters and sorting mint crawlable variants

    Each filter combination on a collection is a URL a crawler can reach and spend its budget on. On a catalogue of any size that is thousands of near-identical pages competing with the one you want ranked.

  3. 03

    One title pattern, hundreds of pages

    Themes build the title and meta description from a template. It is consistent, and it is why the same phrasing repeats across your whole catalogue with nothing distinguishing one page from the next.

  4. 04

    Variant products need ProductGroup

    Google asks for ProductGroup where a product has variants, with price and availability on the variant. Many themes still emit a bare Product and lose the rich result.

  5. 05

    A noindex can arrive by HTTP header

    X-Robots-Tag lives in the response, not in the theme. You can read every template you own and never see the instruction that is keeping a page out of the index.
