The free audit · either track

What lands in your inbox in 48 hours

Not a PDF attachment. A link to a page on this domain that loads in under half a second — a consultant who emails you an 8 MB file has already answered your question. You pick one track; the shape of the report is the same for both.

  1. 01

    The number that hurts

    On speed, what the delay costs you every month, with the formula and coefficient printed underneath. On SEO, how many of the twenty-two checks you fail — a count, not a currency, because revenue lost to indexing cannot be measured from outside without inventing it.

  2. 02

    You against two competitors

    Same test, same day, same connection. Your store beside two others in your niche — because a second in the abstract means nothing, and a second behind a rival means everything. You name the two; guessing them would produce a false comparison.

  3. 03

    Five findings, worst first

    Five. Not twenty. A twenty-point report doesn't get read, it gets postponed. Each one says what's happening, what it costs, and what I'd do — with the metric, the source and the date attached. On the SEO track each finding cites the check it comes from.

  4. 04
    Yours either way

    One fix you can apply today, without me

    Written out to the click: which screen in your Shopify admin, which button, and how to check it worked. If you apply it, see the number move, and never speak to me again, the audit did its job.

  5. 05

    The plan, week by week

    Three weeks on the speed track, four on SEO — the fourth is the re-crawl, because a schema change nobody has re-crawled is deployed, not finished. Either way this is the entire scope of the sprint, written down. Hand it to your own developer if you'd rather; it's yours.

  6. 06

    What happens if nothing changes

    Where the numbers have been drifting and what the next app install will cost. No drama. Slow stores rarely break on any particular day, which is exactly why they stay slow for years.

  7. 07

    One way to reply

    Twenty minutes in a calendar, if you want them. No second CTA, no newsletter, no drip sequence disguised as a resource.

Measured by

PageSpeed Insights for lab data and the CrUX History API for what your real users have been seeing. On the SEO track, your store's own responses — no API keys, nothing you have to grant me.

Every figure carries

Its metric, its value, its source and the date it was taken. A number without those four does not appear — the section renders empty instead.

I read it, check the numbers against the live tools, and record the walkthrough. Nothing is sent that I haven't looked at first.
